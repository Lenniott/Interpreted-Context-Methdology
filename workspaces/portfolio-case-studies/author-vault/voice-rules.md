## What the Voice Is NOT

These patterns break the voice. Avoid them.

**Not performative.** Never announce expertise or seniority. Authority comes from the specificity of the decisions, not from a title drop.

**Not a feature tour.** A case study is not a list of everything that shipped. It is the story of a problem and the judgment used to solve it.

**Not falsely tidy.** Real projects have tradeoffs, dead ends, and constraints. Showing them builds more credibility than a flawless narrative.
