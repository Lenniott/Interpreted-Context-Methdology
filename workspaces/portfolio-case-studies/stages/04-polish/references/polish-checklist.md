# Polish Checklist

The line-level and structural checks to run in the polish stage. Work top to bottom. Pair with the hard constraints in `author-vault/voice-rules.md`.

<!-- Default checklist. Your case study guidance notes may add checks specific to
     your discipline or portfolio standards. Replace or extend as needed. -->

## Structure

- The thesis is clear within the first few lines and holds to the end.
- The strongest material is in the first half, not buried.
- Every section pulls weight. If a section does not change the reader's view of the work, cut or merge it.
- Headings let a skimmer follow the story on their own.

## Evidence

- Every outcome claim is specific and sourced, or honestly hedged.
- Vague impact ("improved," "enhanced," "significantly") is replaced with concrete detail.
- The reader can tell exactly what the author did versus the team.
- Decisions appear with their reasoning and at least one real tradeoff.

## Voice

- Zero violations of the hard constraints in voice-rules.md.
- No hype or marketing language.
- Sentences lead with the point. Specifics over abstractions.
- The opening hooks in one line. The closing reflection is honest and forward-looking.

## Mechanics

- No em dashes. Use a comma, a period, or "--".
- Visual notes are either clear instructions or removed.
- Length is in range for the target set in the case study anatomy.
- Title says what the case study is about, not just the project name.
