# Capture Prompts

The questions that surface the facts a case study needs. Work through these conversationally in the capture stage. Skip what does not apply; chase what produces a strong answer.

<!-- Default prompt set. Your case study guidance notes likely sharpen these or
     add prompts specific to your discipline. Replace or extend as needed. -->

## Context and Role

- What was the project, the product, and the team? When did it happen?
- What was your specific role? What did you personally own versus the team?

## The Problem

- What were you originally asked to do?
- Once you understood the situation, what was the real problem underneath the brief?
- What constraints shaped what was possible (time, budget, tech, politics, users)?

## The Approach

- What were the key decisions? For each, what options did you weigh and why did you choose the one you did?
- What did you try that did not work? What did you learn from it?
- Where did you have to make a tradeoff, and what did you trade away?

## The Outcome

- What happened when it shipped or landed? How do you know?
- What changed for users, the team, or the business? Any numbers?
- What did not go to plan?

## Reflection

- What would you do differently now?
- What did this project teach you about your craft or how you work?

## For Collaboration-Focused Case Studies

- Who did you need to align, and where did you disagree?
- How did you influence a decision you did not control?
